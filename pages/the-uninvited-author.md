The Uninvited Author: AI, Intent, and the Future of Content  provides critical guidance for content operations in the age of artificial intelligence. AI changes the balance of power between authors and readers, and understanding this shift is the key to successful AI content initiatives.

In the rush to AI, many organizations have ignored the product liability, algorithmic bias, and fabrication risks. The Uninvited Author confronts these issues and explores how to navigate the hidden costs of AI for customer-facing and especially regulated content.

Central to the book's argument is the concept of **algorithmic intention** (*intentio algorithmi*) — the goal-directed shaping that an AI system imposes on content as it generates, ranks, or mediates it, apart from what the human author meant or what the reader wants. Where classical hermeneutics worked with the author's intent, the reader's intent, and the intent legible in the work itself, The Uninvited Author argues that a fourth force now shapes meaning before a human ever encounters it: the system's own directional pull.

Recognizing algorithmic intention is what separates organizations that manage AI content risk from those simply surprised by it. The book treats algorithmic bias and fabrication not as isolated failures but as symptoms of an unexamined intentio algorithmi — and shows content operations teams how to identify, govern, and design for that intention rather than discover it after the fact.

Tailored for executives, content professionals, and educators, The Uninvited Author is full of practical advice to build sustainable AI operating models.

The Uninvited Author is indispensable for resilient content operations in the age of AI.
